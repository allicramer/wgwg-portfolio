Searching for funding for our unique invasive algae project! We are combining data from the invasive algae Caulerpa with global and regional oceanographic conditions to forecast invasions into the California Current. 

## Our Partners

We are partnering with the **Port of San Diego** to monitor and develop a *Caulerpa* invasion model. By combining our understanding of oceanographic conditions and *Caulerpa* biology we can forecast invasion events before the algae spreads, aiding regional management efforts at combatinv this destructive species.
